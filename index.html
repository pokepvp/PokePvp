Mining start time and emissions follow the on-chain configuration.

In development

Forge your Founder NFT.

After Genesis mining ends, holders are planned to be able to forge Squads into a Founder NFT by transferring them to a designated address. The number of Squads required will be announced later. Existing mining entitlements are unaffected.

Founder is intended as a long-term credential of participation in PokePvP. Possible benefits include arena access, tournament votes, and priority participation in new seasons.

Specific benefits and rules remain to be announced, and may change or not be implemented. Founder does not promise returns, dividends or price.
